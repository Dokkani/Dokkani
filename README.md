# Dokkani 🎉

Dokkani is a smarter way to turn the things you already own into extra income. If you have cameras, furniture, sports gear, camping equipment, or other items sitting unused, list them, set your own rental prices, and earn money whenever someone needs them.

Instead of letting your valuable items collect dust, give them a second life through a simple, trusted and safe rental experience.

### Why Dokkani?
- **Earn from what you already own**: Turn unused items into a steady stream of income.
- **Set your own pricing**: Decide how much to charge based on your item, demand, and availability.
- **Stay in control**: Review and approve rental requests before any booking is confirmed.
- **Flexible rentals**: Rent out items for weekends, trips, events, or longer-term needs.
- **Support a sharing economy**: Help people access what they need without buying expensive gear outright.

### What Can You Rent Out?
From everyday essentials to specialty items, Dokkani makes it easy to share:
- Cameras and photography gear
- Furniture and home items
- Sports equipment
- Camping and outdoor gear
- Tools and hobby essentials
- Other personal items people want to borrow instead of buy

### How It Works
1. **List your item**: Add a photo, description, and price.
2. **Set availability**: Choose when your item is available to rent.
3. **Review requests**: Approve or decline rental inquiries based on your preferences.
4. **Earn money**: Once approved, your item is rented and you get paid for the time it’s used.

Dokkani helps people share what they have, save money, and make the most of their belongings.
