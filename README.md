# Dokkani

That camera you bought for one trip. The camping tent in the garage. The extra sofa that barely gets used. Sport gear you only need a few weekends a year.

Those things still have value — they just sit idle most of the time.

**Dokkani** is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace that turns unused items into extra income. List what you already own, set your price, and rent it to people nearby who need it for a day, a weekend, or a week.

## The idea

Buying everything you need is expensive. Owning everything you rarely use is wasteful. Dokkani sits in the middle: owners earn money from items that would otherwise collect dust, and renters get access without the cost of buying.

List cameras, furniture, sport gear, camping gear, electronics, clothes, tools, and more. If you own it and you are not using it, someone else probably needs it.

## For owners: make money from what you already have

You stay in control of every listing.

1. **Post an item** — Add a photo, title, description, and pickup address.
2. **Set your price** — Charge a daily rate that matches the item and the market.
3. **Approve rentals** — Review each request and accept only the ones you are comfortable with.
4. **Hand it over, get it back, get paid** — Rent it out for the agreed dates, then list it again.

You decide what goes on the market, how much it costs per day, and who gets to rent it.

## For renters: borrow instead of buy

Need a camera for a shoot, a tent for a camping weekend, or furniture for a short stay? Browse listings, search by name, or filter by category — clothes, household, electronics, and more — then book the item from its owner.

Pay for the days you actually need it. Return it when you are done.

## Why it works

- **Idle items earn money** instead of sitting unused.
- **You set the price** and can change it whenever you want.
- **You approve every rental**, so nothing leaves your hands without your say-so.
- **Renters save money** compared with buying something they only need once.
- **Sharing keeps good stuff in circulation** instead of in storage.

## How a rental works

1. **List or browse** — Owners post items with photos and a daily price. Renters search or filter by category.
2. **Request to rent** — A renter picks dates and sends a request to the owner.
3. **Approve or decline** — The owner reviews the request and decides.
4. **Use and return** — The renter picks up the item, uses it for the agreed period, and returns it.

Start with what you already own. Price it. Approve the first request. That is Dokkani.
